How a change goes live. The Workflow board moves left to right:

  1. Drafts: your work in progress. New entries start here.
  2. In review: move the card here when it's ready for a second look. A preview link builds automatically.
  3. Ready: move it here once it's approved.
  4. Publish: hit Publish and the live site rebuilds in a minute or two.

Photos: if an uploaded image stays blank in this editor, don't worry, it still saves and appears on the live site.
